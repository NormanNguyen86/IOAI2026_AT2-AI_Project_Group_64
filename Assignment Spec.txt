## 2. Specification page (verbatim)


### **Intent**:


This assignment equips you with AI thinking and critical analysis of various phases in undertaking AI projects, including defining problem statement, assessing and selecting proper AI techniques, and developing methodologies, creating system architectures, processes and workflow, and evaluation criteria. The task trains you to search and integrate domain-specific knowledge, problem context, data and resources with AI techniques to design desirable solutions. The proposal integrates cross-disciplinary thinking, knowledge, and skills for data-driven evidence-based design to solve an interesting and challenging real-world problem.


### **Objective: **


This assessment task addresses the subject learning objectives SLO3 and SLO4.


### **Course intended learning outcome(s):**


C.1 and E.1


### **Weighting:**


35%


### **Group Work:**


Group, group and individually assessed


### **Due date: **


Both parts of this assessment task are due by 11:59PM, Friday, 23/10/2026


### **Task**


This is a two-part assessment task.


In Part 1, you need to work in a small group to develop a written report that includes detailed design of a solution for solving _a significant real-world problem that affects society in a large scale, such as climate change, public health and education.  You can let your imagination fly and formulate a problem that has non-trivial societal impact and can be solved by chosen AI techniques. Each member in your team should explore one AI technique for solving the target problem. The report should include problem statement, background and motivation, specific objectives, data resources, solution designs including AI technique selection and justification, development process, expected outcomes, evaluation, and risk management, etc. A report template is available [here](https://canvas.uts.edu.au/courses/40998/files/12438437?verifier=1lYhehoSgtV8WMFqJXahlgrXh0n43dLuUc06ebUU&wrap=1). _


_Your group also needs to produce a presentation video for end-users of their AI solution._


In Part 2, you need to write a reflection report to present evidence that reflect what you have learned from this task, such as the AI application domain, challenges of the target problem and why selected AI technique can help, what contribution you have made to design the solution in Part 1 and what roles you were able to take if the proposed solution would be implemented. **In addition, in this reflection report, you also include a peer review for each of the other group members**. A template is available [here](https://canvas.uts.edu.au/courses/40998/files/12438333?verifier=Bp1ji0UD5HXFv5Vx0VGXhTcmzBLxvV6mGzUBN5cd&wrap=1).


**Note. A weekly team progress log could be useful for collecting evidence of project development. **


### **Deliverables:**


1. A group report. length is 15- 20 pages.


2. Group presentation video. The video length is no longer than 15 minutes. All members should be in the video.


3. An individual reflection report.  Length is 3- 5 pages.


**Notes: Each page refers to a standard A4 page with standard margins and single line spacing using Times New Roman, font-12.  **


### **Reference style:**


UTS APA referencing style.


### **Mark allocations:**


Part 1 weights 27 marks, including 12 marks for the report and 15 marks for video presentation; Part 2 weights 8 marks.


### **Submission requirements**:


Each submission will need to be submitted to the designated folders in the [assignments page](https://canvas.uts.edu.au/courses/40998/assignments) i.e. AT2 Part A and Part B.


### **File name conventions**:


Part 1, TeamNumber_AT2_report.pdf (or.docx) and TeamNumber_AT2_video.mp4 (or .mov).


**Notes**: Part 1 is a group submission. Each group needs to have one team member to upload their AT2 AI Project Plan and Design report and video presentation by the due date.


Part 2, YourSurname_YourFirstname_AT2_reflection.docx (.pdf)


### **Feedback:**


** **Summative feedback will be provided with the marks within 2-4 weeks after the due date.


### **Suggestions of application areas:**


1. Smart education system using AI + VR + cloud resource


2. Smart city, such as smart traffic lights management to be adaptive to traffic flow


3. Smart elderly care using smart sensors and AI techniques


4. Smart transport service system using autonomous/self-driving cars


5. Smart solar energy sharing system among neighbourhood using solar PV panels and AI technique


6. Smart online shopping platform using VR


7. Smart remote medical diagnosis and treatment recommendation

Summary or Executive Summary
This section briefly summarises the purpose of the project, major findings and conclusions. It is designed for those who do not have the time to read the entire report and as a means of refreshing a reader's mind at some later time. It is probably the most important section of the report, because it is almost certain to be read. The summary can be up to one page.


Introduction
The Introduction is an extremely important part of any report. It should not include too much detail, but it should give the reader a good idea of where the report is going (including a brief description of the contents of the report).


This section need include the following subheadings:
Business context
The part presents a logical and informative background and clear-defined business objectives.


Problem statement
This part should address the following issues:
what was the problem and its context,
why was it a problem, and
Why is the project necessary and
How will the problem be solved (briefly).


Note. The problem in this assignment should be a significant problem that affects society in a large scale, such as climate change, public health and education. You can let your imagination fly and design a problem that can be solved by AI techniques (not necessarily the ones covered in this subject)


Project Proposal
Project aims and Objectives
This part presents the project aims and project objectives to achieve the business objectives.


Methodology
This part presents the tasks to achieve the project objectives. Each task is a piece of work that needs certain time to complete (i.e., associated time allocation). For each task, explain clearly what it is, what the expected results including the milestones from completing this task and how this task contributes to the project objective.


Expected outcomes
This part presents what this project will deliver upon its completion.


Benefits and significance of this project
This part presents the benefits from this project and significance of the expected outcomes.


Resources
This part presents all the resources that could be used for the project, including personnel (project team formation with all required roles and responsibilities of these roles, computational resources, data acquisition / purchase costs, etc.




Risks
This part presents all the risk factors that influence the project from technical to social aspects, such as risk factors related to personnel, data collection and computational power and deployment environment.


Timeline
This part presents the time frame required to complete this project and time allocation to each of the tasks.


Communication of expected outcomes
This part presents the plan of how to disseminate the expected outcomes to generate some benefits and/or social impacts.




Solution design
For each task listed in the previous section, how will this task be carried out, including what resources are needed, what AI techniques can be considered and how these selected AI techniques work in this problem context, and what are the selection criteria for determination of the technique that will be used in the solution, how to produce the expected outcomes and how to evaluate the results.


Implementation design
This part presents the detailed design of how the tasks listed in the proposal will be implemented, including the potential development environment, libraries, tools, and/or frameworks that could be used to carry out the tasks, and AI technique selection and justification.


Evaluation design
This part presents the design of evaluation strategies for validating the solution.


Risk management
This part presents the risk management plan which includes the strategies that address all risk factors listed in the proposal section.


List of References
When you use material from other reports or books, you must properly acknowledge it, by citing the report or book in a list of references, and referring to the list at the places in the text where the material is used. Please use The APA (American Psychological Association) referencing style in this report.
Citations and References are important parts of writing in an academic environment.


Appendices
Detailed material that is not essential to, but supports the information contained in the body of the report, should be placed in an appendix. Appropriate appendix materials include supporting data, detailed explanations of methodologies or procedures, derivation of formulas, conversion factors, and data collection formats, protocols, and checklists. Appendices are a powerful means of fully documenting and supporting the results of the study without cluttering the body of the report.
